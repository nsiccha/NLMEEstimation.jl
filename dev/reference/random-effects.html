Random effects ​

Layouts and coordinates ​

NLMEEstimation.NLMELayout Type
julia
NLMELayout(; ntheta, nsigma, eta_blocks)

Parameter layout of an NLME model, returned by nlme_layout.

  • ntheta: length of θ, the population (fixed-effect) coordinates.

  • nsigma: length of σ, the other scalar coordinates the conditional density depends on (residual-error parameters, for example).

  • eta_blocks: sizes of the mutually independent blocks of the per-subject random-effect vector η_i, in η order. sum(eta_blocks)is the η dimension (neta). A diagonal Ω isones(Int, neta); one dense Ω is[neta].

θ and σ are in the model's own unconstrained coordinates: every real vector of the right length is a valid input to conditional_loglikelihood.

Ω is owned by this package. Its unconstrained parameter vector ω is the concatenation, block by block, of the log-Cholesky coordinates of each block Ω_b = L_b L_bᵀ: the entries of the lower triangle of L_b in row-major order (L[1,1], L[2,1], L[2,2], L[3,1], …), with each diagonal entry stored as its logarithm. For a block of size one, the coordinate is log(sd). nomega gives the length of ω.

source
NLMEEstimation.RaggedNLMELayout Type
julia
RaggedNLMELayout(; ntheta, nsigma, family_sizes, subject_families)

A nested random-effect layout with one shared covariance per family. family_sizes[f] is the dimension of family f; subject_families[i] lists the family of each successive block in subject i's η vector. A family may occur more than once in a subject (for example, once per lesion). No absent block is padded into η. The model's subject order must agree with subject_families.

Fixed covariance ​

NLMEEstimation.FixedOmega Type
julia
FixedOmega(Ω::AbstractMatrix)
FixedOmega(; sd = nothing, correlation = nothing)

A known value for all or part of one random-effect covariance family, declared through fixed_omega. FixedOmega(Ω) holds the whole family at the covariance matrix Ω, like nlmixr2's fix() on an ω block or NONMEM's OMEGA … FIX.

The keyword form fixes parts of a family in standard-deviation and correlation terms, Ω = D R D with D = Diagonal(sd):

  • sd is nothing (every margin estimated) or a vector with one entry per margin: a positive standard deviation for a fixed margin, and nothing or missing for an estimated one;

  • correlation is nothing (estimated) or the known correlation matrix R, symmetric positive definite with unit diagonal.

The estimators fit the remaining parts with the fixed parts held at their values. A family of size one has only its standard deviation.

source
NLMEEstimation.fixed_omega Function
julia
fixed_omega(model) -> nothing, or family => FixedOmega pairs

Optional declaration that some random-effect covariance families of model are known, wholly or in part. Return nothing (the default) when every family is estimated, or a collection of family => FixedOmega(…) pairs (a vector of pairs, a single pair, or a dictionary). A family is an entry of the layout's eta_blocks (NLMELayout) or family_sizes (RaggedNLMELayout), by position.

focei and saem estimate only the free parts and keep the fixed parts at their values, so the fitted omega and omega_matrix report them. Their starting ω0 must already hold the fixed values; apply_fixed_omega builds such a vector. empirical_bayes evaluates at whatever ω it is given, and check_protocol verifies that its ω holds the declared values.

source
NLMEEstimation.apply_fixed_omega Function
julia
apply_fixed_omega(model, ω) -> Vector

The Ω parameters ω with each family declared by fixed_omega moved onto its fixed values. The estimated parts of a constrained family keep their values in ω, in standard-deviation and correlation terms: an estimated margin keeps its standard deviation, and an estimated correlation keeps the correlation of ω. Unconstrained families are unchanged.

NLMEEstimation.NormalREPrior Type
julia
NormalREPrior(layout::NLMELayout, ω)

The random-effect prior η_i ~ N(0, Ω) with Ω = omega_matrix(layout, ω), with its Cholesky factor, precision and log-determinant computed once for repeated per-subject evaluation. This package owns the η prior; models never implement it.

julia
complete_data_loglikelihood(model, i, θ, σ, ω_or_prior, η) -> Real

The complete-data log-density log p(y_i, η_i | θ, σ, Ω) = conditional_loglikelihood(model, i, θ, σ, η) + re_logprior(prior, η). The fifth argument is either the Ω parameter vector ω or a precomputed NormalREPrior.
