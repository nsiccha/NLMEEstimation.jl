# Test data

- `theoph.csv`: R's `datasets::Theoph` (theophylline after a single oral dose in 12
  subjects; Boeckmann, Sheiner and Beal, *NONMEM Users Guide*, 1994), exported with
  R 4.5.2. `Dose` is in mg/kg, so the dose amount is `Dose * Wt` mg; `conc` is in mg/L
  and `Time` in hours.
- `nlmixr2_posthoc_theoph.csv`: nlmixr2 posthoc (empirical Bayes) estimates of
  `eta.ka`, `eta.cl`, `eta.v` per subject on `nlmixr2data::theo_sd` (identical to
  `theoph.csv`) at the fixed parameters of `test/common.jl`, generated by
  `test/oracles/nlmixr2_posthoc_theoph.R` with nlmixr2 7.0.1, nlmixr2est 7.1.0,
  rxode2 5.1.7.1 and R 4.5.2. A test-only oracle: the suite compares against this
  file and needs no R.
- `nlmixr2_posthoc_theoph_predictions.csv`: from the same script, nlmixr2's
  individual predictions at its EBEs (`IPRED`) and typical-value predictions (`PRED`)
  per observation.
- `saemix_saem_theoph.csv`: saemix 3.5 estimates on `theoph.csv`, generated by
  `test/oracles/saemix_theoph.R` with R 4.5.2. The script uses the same oral
  one-compartment model, CL weight exponent, diagonal Ω, and additive residual
  error as `test/common.jl`. It is a test-only SAEM oracle; the suite needs no R.
- `nlmixr2_focei_theoph_estimates.csv` and `nlmixr2_focei_theoph_cov_{rs,r,s}.csv`:
  nlmixr2 FOCEi estimates on `theoph.csv` (`tka`, `tcl`, `tv`, `add.sd`, the Ω
  variances, and the objective `OBJF`) and the full covariance matrices of its
  covariance methods `"r,s"`, `"r"` and `"s"` (`covFull = TRUE`), for the
  one-compartment oral model with additive error and diagonal Ω of
  `nlmixr2_posthoc_theoph.R`. Generated by `test/oracles/nlmixr2_covariance_theoph.R`
  with nlmixr2 7.0.1, nlmixr2est 7.1.0, rxode2 5.1.7.1 and R 4.5.2; test-only,
  compared in `test/covariance.jl`.
- `saemix_loglik_theoph.csv`: saemix 3.5 estimates on `theoph.csv` (the same fit
  as `saemix_saem_theoph.csv`), with its log-likelihood by importance sampling
  (`ll.is`, 50,000 draws) and Gauss–Hermite quadrature (`ll.gq`, 12 nodes), the
  matching AIC/BIC, and the observation and subject counts. Generated by
  `test/oracles/saemix_loglik_theoph.R` with R 4.5.2; test-only, compared in
  `test/marginal.jl`.
- `nlmixr2_residuals_theoph.csv`: nlmixr2's FOCEi residual table on `theoph.csv`
  (DV, PRED, IPRED, RES, IRES, IWRES, WRES, CPRED, CRES, CWRES, and from
  `addNpde` with 300 simulations EPRED, NPDE, NPD, PDE, PD), for the model and
  estimates of `nlmixr2_focei_theoph_estimates.csv`. Generated by
  `test/oracles/nlmixr2_residuals_theoph.R` with nlmixr2 7.0.1, nlmixr2est 7.1.0,
  rxode2 5.1.7.1 and R 4.5.2; test-only, compared in `test/diagnostics.jl`.
- `nlmixr2_saem_theoph_estimates.csv` and `nlmixr2_saem_theoph_cov_sa{500,5000}.csv`:
  nlmixr2 SAEM estimates on `theoph.csv` (`tka`, `tcl`, `tv`, `add.sd`, the Ω
  variances) and the full covariance of its default SAEM covariance method `"sa"`
  (Louis's identity in a covariance phase at the estimate; `covFull = TRUE`) with
  500 (the default) and 5,000 covariance-phase iterations, for the model of
  `nlmixr2_covariance_theoph.R`. Generated by `test/oracles/nlmixr2_saem_sa_theoph.R`
  with nlmixr2 7.0.1, nlmixr2est 7.1.0, rxode2 5.1.7.1 and R 4.5.2; test-only,
  compared in `test/louis.jl`.
